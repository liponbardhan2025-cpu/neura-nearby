DATA SOURCES

  • Indian stock quotes and charts: Yahoo Finance (NSE and BSE, delayed about 15 minutes). Market headlines: The Economic Times and The Hindu.
  • Places and maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), served through Overpass and Nominatim. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors, Humanitarian OSM Team and Esri.
  • Weather, air quality, elevation: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Popular places: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).
  • Aircraft positions: community ADS-B data from adsb.lol (ODbL), adsb.fi and adsb.one, with OpenSky Network as backup. No API key needed.
  • Earthquakes: USGS. Space weather: NOAA SWPC. Space station: wheretheiss.at.
  • Exchange rates: ExchangeRate-API.
  • Hazards: NASA EONET. News and history: Wikipedia. Tech stories: Hacker News (Algolia). Space news: Spaceflight News API. Launches: The Space Devs Launch Library 2. Crypto: CoinGecko. Country data: REST Countries. Holidays: Nager.Date.
  • Train data: the provider you connect with your own key.

PRIVACY

There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Your location is used only to request nearby data from the services above, which see your request like any website would. Your transit board, followed trains, units and API key are stored only in this browser. “Clear my data” removes them.

TERMS OF USE

This app is provided as is, for general information. Map data can be incomplete or out of date. Timetables you enter are yours, and live feeds can be delayed. Do not rely on it for emergencies, navigation safety or aviation. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
