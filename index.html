PRIVACY

There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Your location is used only to request nearby data from the services above, which see your request like any website would. Your transit board, followed trains, units and API key are stored only in this browser. “Clear my data” removes them.

TERMS OF USE

This app is provided as is, for general information. Map data can be incomplete or out of date. Timetables you enter are yours, and live feeds can be delayed. Do not rely on it for emergencies, navigation safety or aviation. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
